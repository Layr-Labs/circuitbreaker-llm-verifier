# Exact score and current transport limits
The mathematical finite score is reduced p/q. The protected checker accepts at most
256 decimal digits per integer as a runtime resource bound. Larger encodings fail
closed; this is not a mathematical disproof or an endpoint. The exact rational lives
in the dossier; binary64 transport/display is approximate and can collapse distinct
scores. Official comparison uses cross multiplication. There is no normalization.

For an actually proved superlinear bound for one fixed eligible family the semantic
score is positive infinity and the first challenge is complete. The tagged terminal
record distinguishes it from a lemma. Current platform transport remains scores:null
with explicit finite-only limitation; platform leaderboard endpoint ordering is not
implemented. No large finite sentinel, NaN or numeric Infinity is permitted.

The canonical metadata and proof chunks are supplied in a required checker input.
The protected checker cannot read candidate files. The model must faithfully compare
these inputs with the submitted theorem/proof, and explicitly accept the exact
checker-derived claim ID. SHA256 binding and deterministic arithmetic do not verify
the theorem or eliminate model extraction errors. Official promotion needs human
review of exact artifacts. Individual typed strings are <=4096 characters and all
checker inputs are <=16KiB; oversized proofs are explicitly unsupported by this
initial canonical-input format and must not be silently truncated or replaced by an
unreviewed digest. Extending proof transport requires a reviewed contract change.
