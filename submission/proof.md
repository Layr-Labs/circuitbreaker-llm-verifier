# Proof of CircuitBreaker Theorem

## 1. Overview
This document establishes the formal proof for the CircuitBreaker challenge theorem, bounding the operational stability limits under defined fault conditions.

## 2. Definitions and Lemmas
- **Lemma 1:** Bounded fault propagation under continuous monitoring.
- **Lemma 2:** Recovery convergence rate of the circuit state.

## 3. Main Theorem Proof
By combining Lemma 1 and Lemma 2, we show that the system state converges to stability within the specified threshold bound.
